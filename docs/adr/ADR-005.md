# ADR-005: Solo worker by default

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

A small request produces one visible lead bot. Specialists appear only when
the delegation conditions in `plugin/skills/visual-team/references/
delegation-rules.md` are met. Maximum three visible bots; one active writer.

## Rationale

Consumers find a fake team more confusing than helpful, and parallel agents
raise cost and write-conflict risk.

## Consequences

- The `start_visual_task` contract enforces the cap and the single writer.
- Eval metrics track that ≥90% of small tasks stay solo.
