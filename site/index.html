What happened, and how we know

Evidence labels
are part of the product.

Native hooks can record activity. The agent can report a phase or result. Visual Team keeps those sources distinct, including when hooks are unavailable.

A “passed” report is the agent’s account of its checks. It is not independent proof that the whole product is correct.

Observed

Recorded native lifecycle activity.

Reported

A phase, question, outcome, or check the agent reports.

Derived

A calculated hint, such as no recent activity.
